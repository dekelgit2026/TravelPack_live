# Harbor & Pine: travel policy (excerpt)

**T1. Booking window.** Book at least **14 days** ahead. A booking made later needs a reason, and the manager approves it if the fare is more than 20% above the lowest fare.

**T2. Cabin.** Economy for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class needs VP approval in advance.

**T3. Fare choice.** Choose the lowest logical fare: at most one stop, and no more than 3 hours longer than the fastest option. Preferred airlines are fine when they cost no more than 10% above the lowest logical fare.

**T4. Hotels.** The nightly cap is **$350** in Tier 1 cities (London, New York, Tel Aviv) and **$220** in Tier 2 cities, before tax. Prefer the company's preferred hotels within 3 km of the meeting place.

**T5. Saturday-night stays.** These are allowed when they lower the total trip cost, including the hotel. Otherwise the employee pays the extra nights.

**T6. Duty of care.** Destinations on the **security watch list** need security team approval before booking. Never book these automatically.

**T7. Personal data.** Passport, date of birth and loyalty numbers stay in the employee's traveller profile in the booking tool. They are never shared by email or chat.

**T8. Approvals.** The manager approves any trip out of policy (T1–T5). A coordinator books only after the needed approvals are recorded.

**T9. Changes.** A change fee above $200 needs the manager's OK.
